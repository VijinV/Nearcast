# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want Nearcast to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or identity. We pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience for all.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully, and keep criticism focused on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling or deliberate derailing.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews), and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers. Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban a contributor whose behaviour they consider harmful. Depending on how serious the problem is, steps range from a private warning to a permanent ban.
